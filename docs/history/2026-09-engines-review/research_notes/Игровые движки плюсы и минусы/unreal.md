# Unreal Engine (UE5): praise, complaints, Epic's own admissions (as of Sept 2026)

## 1. What developers praise

### Takeaway
Praise centres on best-in-class out-of-the-box high-end rendering, Blueprints enabling tiny programming teams to ship large games (Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 is the 2025-26 poster child), and a royalty model that is free until $1M. Epic keeps shipping large performance/feature upgrades (5.7 foliage, 5.8 shader reduction).

### Cited Findings
- Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 (Sandfall, ~30 devs, 2025 GOTY-calibre hit) was "95 percent" built with Blueprints — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/programming/clair-obscur-expedition-33-was-built-95-percent-with-unreal-blueprints)
- GDC 2026 talk by Sandfall CTO Tom Guillermin: "Delivering a Wide Scope of Features & Content When You Only Have Four Programmers"; Blueprints used "for ALL gameplay systems"; "vanilla-first" approach: "We take all the tools that are natively present and we push them to their limits so we don't have to develop bespoke tools" ; whole team became "Blueprint-fluent" and contributed to logic — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/sandfall-interactive-used-ue5-blueprints-for-all-gameplay-systems-in-clair-obscur)
- Epic's own interviews with Sandfall on why UE5 was key — [Unreal Engine](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/inside-the-development-journey-of-clair-obscur-expedition-33); [ixbt.games, Jul 2026](https://ixbt.games/en/news/2026/07/29/425671-avtory-clair-obscur-expedition-33-obieiasnili-pocemu-unreal-engine-5-stal-kliucom-k-uspexu-igry.html)
- UE 5.7: GPU performance up to ~25% and CPU up to ~35% in a comparison of the same demo on 5.4 vs 5.7, with "more stable frametimes with less hitches" — [Wccftech](https://wccftech.com/with-significant-gpu-and-cpu-performance-improvements-unreal-engine-5-7-is-another-step-in-the-right-direction/); [GameGPU](https://en.gamegpu.com/News/zhelezo/Unreal-Engine-5.7-speeds-up-UE5-and-reduces-stuttering-on-the-PC)
- ARK: Survival Ascended developer: UE 5.7 is "as close to a magic bullet" for performance (esp. Nanite foliage), but hitching in a game that doesn't precompile all shaders "is always going to exist to some extent" — [Wccftech](https://wccftech.com/ue-5-7-is-close-to-a-magic-bullet-for-performance-says-ark-developer-though-it-wont-fully-eliminate-stutters/)
- CD Projekt Red + Epic: Witcher 4 UE5 tech demo (Unreal Fest 2025) running on base PS5 at 60 fps with ray tracing; co-developed Fast Geometry Streaming plugin and Nanite Foliage contributed back to the engine — [CD Projekt Red blog](https://www.cdprojektred.com/en/blog/149/working-with-epic-to-debut-the-witcher-4-unreal-engine-5-tech-demo-at-unreal-fest); [GameGPU on FastGeo](https://en.gamegpu.com/news/igry/cd-projekt-red-i-epic-games-razrabotali-plagin-fast-geo-streaming-dlya-ustraneniya-statterov-v-unreal-engine-5-6)
- UE 5.8 (released at Unreal Fest / State of Unreal, June 2026): optimized shader compilation — on Fortnite shader count dropped 68% with faster cooking, loading and less memory; experimental MCP plugin for connecting AI models (e.g. Claude) to UE projects — [Inven Global on X](https://x.com/InvenGlobal/status/2067471772883071016); [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/unreal-engine-6-ue5-uefn-merge-for-fortnite-creators-in-2026); [Epic: State of Unreal 2026](https://www.unrealengine.com/news/state-of-unreal-2026-top-news-from-the-show) (Epic page returned 403 to fetch; details via secondary sources)
- Royalty model: free for games; 5% royalty only on lifetime gross revenue over $1M — [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/epic-confirms-its-new-unreal-engine-pricing-keeps-its-promise-not-to-change-it-for-game-developers/); [Unreal license page](https://www.unrealengine.com/license)
- Fab marketplace: 88/12 revenue share for creators (100% on Standard-licensed sales from launch to end of 2024) — [Fab on X](https://twitter.com/fab/status/1846525551399330058); [CG Channel](https://www.cgchannel.com/2024/10/epic-games-launches-its-new-fab-marketplace-in-october-2024/)

### Inferences
- The Clair Obscur case is now the go-to counterargument to "Blueprint spaghetti / UE needs big C++ teams": a small team shipped a hit largely vanilla.
- The Witcher 4 partnership functions both as praise (AAA studio switching) and as Epic's de facto admission that open-world streaming needed engine-level work.

## 2. Top recurring complaints

### Takeaway
The dominant complaint through 2024-2026 is PC performance: shader compilation stutter (largely mitigated by PSO precaching when used properly) and traversal/streaming stutter (still present, DF calls it an engine problem even in Fortnite), plus heavy hardware demands and temporal blur/ghosting (TAA/TSR + Lumen denoising). "UE5 = runs badly" has become a player meme.

### Cited Findings
- Digital Foundry on Silent Hill 2 remake PC (Oct 2024): no shader-comp stutter but traversal stutter from loading/unloading objects; framed as an engine issue Epic hadn't resolved even in Fortnite; headline "#StutterStruggle Cannot Be Avoided" — [Wccftech](https://wccftech.com/silent-hill-2-remake-pc-is-yet-another-unreal-engine-case-of-stutter-struggle/); [ResetEra DF thread](https://www.resetera.com/threads/df-silent-hill-2-remake-pc-visuals-scale-beyond-ps5-but-stutterstruggle-cannot-be-avoided.1008156/)
- Digital Foundry on Oblivion Remastered PC (Apr 2025) — "Impressive Remastering, Dire Performance Problems"; Alex Battaglia called it one of the worst-running games he had tested; hitching even on the fastest consumer CPU; switching from hardware to software Lumen gave ~35% more performance — [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Tech-analysis-slams-The-Elder-Scrolls-IV-Oblivion-Remastered-performance-for-frame-rate-drops-on-top-hardware.1005870.0.html); [ResetEra](https://www.resetera.com/threads/digital-foundry-oblivion-remastered-pc-impressive-remastering-dire-performance-problems.1174488/); DF said popular stutter-fix mods don't fix it — [OC3D](https://overclock3d.net/news/software/digital-foundry-rubbishes-popular-oblivion-stutter-fix-mods/). A later patch reportedly worsened stutter — [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Oblivion-Remastered-performance-worsens-after-patch-leading-to-stutters-and-mixed-Steam-reviews.1108215.0.html)
- Borderlands 4 (Sept 2025, UE5): DF — "Yes, There are Big Issues" on consoles, frame rate in performance mode degrades from ~60 to high-40s/low-50s after 30-60 min — [ResetEra DF thread](https://www.resetera.com/threads/digital-foundry-borderlands-4-yes-there-are-big-issues-ps5-ps5-pro-xbox-series-x-s.1302756/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borderlands_4). Mixed Steam reviews; Gearbox CEO Randy Pitchford said the game is "very optimal", "Every PC gamer must accept the reality of the relationship between their hardware and what the software they are running is doing", sarcastically told critics to "code their own engine", and to refund if unhappy — [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/borderlands-4-ceo-code-your-own-engine); [KitGuru](https://www.kitguru.net/gaming/matthew-wilson/randy-pitchford-goes-to-war-against-pc-gamers-following-borderlands-4-criticism/)
- Metal Gear Solid Delta and Wuchang: Fallen Feathers (2025) cited as UE5 titles with significant performance problems, fuelling "blame UE5" sentiment — [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/epic-games-ceo-tim-sweeney-explains-unreal-engine-5-performance-problems); [Notebookcheck on MGS3 remake](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Metal-Gear-Solid-3-remake-stuttering-has-more-gamers-blaming-Unreal-Engine-5-for-poor-performance.1095493.0.html)
- Temporal blur / ghosting / Lumen noise: recurring player complaints on Steam forums across UE5 titles (e.g. Stalker 2: "UE5 - Ghosting, blur and noise") — [Steam, Stalker 2](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1643320/discussions/0/7051042840464422178/); [Steam, Clair Obscur](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1903340/discussions/0/592897077193749232) (forum sentiment, not authoritative)
- Borderlands 4 stutter causes as commonly diagnosed: shader compilation, open-world traversal streaming, Lumen/textures overrunning VRAM — [Tier1Settings](https://tier1settings.com/borderlands-4-stuttering-fix/) (low-authority guide site)

### Inferences
- Shader-compilation stutter is increasingly a "studio didn't do it properly" issue (PSO precaching available since 5.1-5.3), while traversal stutter is more genuinely engine-level; this is the key nuance for the report.
- Player backlash often targets the engine brand even where studio decisions (hardware Lumen defaults, no precompile step) are the proximate cause.

## 3. What Epic has officially acknowledged

### Takeaway
Epic has openly acknowledged stutter as a problem (2025 tech blog + Inside Unreal stream, 2026 Unreal Fest), while Tim Sweeney publicly places the main blame on studios' development order (Aug 2025). UE6 (early access late 2027) will merge UEFN/Verse workflows; Epic had two large layoffs (2023, 2026).

### Cited Findings
- Epic tech blog "Game engines and shader stuttering: Unreal Engine's solution to the problem" (by Kenzo ter Elst, Daniele Vettorel, Allan Bentham, Mihnea Balta; published early Feb 2025, not 2024) explains shader-compilation stutter ("everything stops while waiting for the driver to finish compilation"), PSO precaching evolution, best practices; notes solutions for memory impact and deciding which precached PSOs to retain "already in development" — [Unreal Engine tech blog](https://www.unrealengine.com/tech-blog/game-engines-and-shader-stuttering-unreal-engines-solution-to-the-problem); [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/epic-games-devs-on-solutions-to-unreal-engine-5-shader-stuttering-issues); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/hardware/graphics-cards/epic-talks-shop-about-stuttering-in-games-that-use-its-unreal-engine-and-offers-solutions-to-the-problem/); Inside Unreal stream Feb 6, 2025 — [Epic forums](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/inside-unreal-game-engines-shader-stuttering-unreal-engine-s-solution/2317481)
- PSO precaching was introduced in UE 5.1 (historical) — [DSOGaming](https://www.dsogaming.com/news/epic-games-adds-new-pso-precaching-mechanism-to-unreal-engine-5-1-to-reduce-shader-compilation-stutters/)
- Tim Sweeney, Unreal Fest Seoul (Aug 28, 2025): "Many developers develop games for high-end hardware, then perform optimization and testing on lower-spec devices in the final stages of development." Epic response: "We're doing two things: strengthening engine support with more automated optimization across devices, and expanding developer education so 'optimize early' becomes standard practice. If needed, our engineers can step in." Also porting Fortnite low-end optimization lessons into the engine — [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/unreal-engine-5-performance-issues-are-mainly-due-to-devs-not-optimising-properly-epic-ceo-tim-sweeney-says/); [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/epic-games-ceo-tim-sweeney-explains-unreal-engine-5-performance-problems); [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/109267-performance-woes-unreal-engine-5-games-developers-fault.html). Community reaction: seen as deflecting blame — [ResetEra](https://www.resetera.com/threads/tim-sweeney-on-ue5-optimization-issues-%E2%80%9Cmany-studios-build-for-top-tier-hardware-first-and-leave-optimization-and-low-spec-testing-for-the-end.1281909/)
- Epic's Marcus Wassmer (June 2026): developers told Epic "The features are great, but we want better optimization for actual performance," i.e. fewer frame drops and stutter — [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/22928/ceo-tim-sweeney-unreal-engine-6-will-fundamentally-change-the-development-paradigm)
- Epic reiterated working with CDPR to eliminate UE5 stutter — [Tech4Gamers](https://tech4gamers.com/epic-games-working-with-cdpr-unreal-engine5-stuttering/)
- UE6: announced at Unreal Fest/State of Unreal June 2026; merges UE5 and UEFN into one tool with Verse; Early Access targeted late 2027, stable ~2028; UE 5.8 is the last planned major UE5 release (5.9 possible) — [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/22900/unreal-engine-6-to-feature-fundamental-overhaul-targeting-early-access-release-by-late-2027); [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/unreal-engine-6-ue5-uefn-merge-for-fortnite-creators-in-2026); [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/epic-games-officially-teases-unreal-engine-6)
- Sweeney on Verse vs C++ (June 2026): Verse designed for a "massive interconnected" shared codebase; transactional memory to make data races impossible; JavaScript analogy — millions of devs in one shared codebase without a type system and "the entire system would explode" — [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/22928/ceo-tim-sweeney-unreal-engine-6-will-fundamentally-change-the-development-paradigm)
- Layoffs Sept 2023: ~830 staff (~16%); Sweeney: "we've been spending way more money than we earn"; "I had long been optimistic that we could power through this transition without layoffs, but in retrospect I see that this was unrealistic"; ~2/3 of cuts outside core development; Unreal Engine listed among core businesses not impacted — [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2023/09/28/tech/epic-games-layoffs-fortnite/index.html); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/epic-games-is-laying-off-more-than-800-people/)
- Layoffs March 25, 2026: 1,000+ staff (~20%); Sweeney: "We're spending significantly more than we're making", market conditions "the most extreme we've seen since those early days"; Fortnite playtime decline cited; >$500M savings target; impact on UE team not stated — [heise](https://www.heise.de/en/news/Epic-Games-lays-off-around-20-percent-of-its-workforce-with-over-1000-employees-11223291.html); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/epic-games-lays-off-more-than-1-000-employees-were-spending-significantly-more-than-were-making-ceo-tim-sweeney-says/)

### Inferences
- Epic's messaging is two-track: engineering acknowledges stutter as real and ships fixes (PSO precaching, FastGeo, 5.7/5.8 perf), while the CEO frames the problem as studio process.
- UE6's Verse pivot signals Epic sees C++/Blueprint as insufficient long term; a migration burden for studios is likely (inference, not stated by Epic).

## 4. Business: royalties, licensing, Fab

### Takeaway
Games: 5% over $1M lifetime gross, unchanged (Epic kept its promise). Non-games (film, auto, simulation) moved to $1,850/seat/year in 2024 for companies over $1M revenue. Fab replaced Unreal Marketplace/Quixel/ArtStation/Sketchfab markets in Oct 2024 with 88/12 split but a messy Megascans transition.

### Cited Findings
- Seat-based pricing (announced 2023, effective late April 2024 with UE 5.4): $1,850 per seat for non-game uses (linear content/film/TV, automotive, theme parks etc.); free under $1M annual gross revenue and for students/educators/hobbyists; games remain 5% over $1M — [Epic blog](https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US/blog/we-are-updating-unreal-engine-twinmotion-and-realitycapture-pricing-in-late-april); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/epic-will-charge-non-game-developers-1850-per-seat-to-use-unreal-engine-162015997.html); [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/epic-confirms-its-new-unreal-engine-pricing-keeps-its-promise-not-to-change-it-for-game-developers/)
- Fab launched mid-Oct 2024 replacing UE Marketplace, Quixel Megascans, ArtStation and Sketchfab stores; 88% creator revenue share — [CG Channel](https://www.cgchannel.com/2024/10/epic-games-launches-its-new-fab-marketplace-in-october-2024/); [Epic blog](https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US/blog/fab-content-marketplace-launches-in-october-publishing-portal-opens-today)
- Megascans free-for-all ended with 2024, most content moving to paid in 2025; Fab lacked Quixel Bridge features, and after community frustration Epic restored Bridge/Quixel.com access to legacy library through Dec 31, 2024 — [GameFromScratch](https://gamefromscratch.com/quixel-megascans-is-back/); [Wikipedia: Fab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fab_(website))

### Inferences
- Royalty model remains a genuine competitive advantage vs. Unity's 2023 runtime-fee fiasco (context from other researchers).
