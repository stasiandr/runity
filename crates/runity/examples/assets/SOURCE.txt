teapot.obj — the Utah/Newell teapot

Original model: Martin Newell, 1975 (public domain; the canonical
computer-graphics test object, with no known copyright holder or
restriction on reuse).

Downloaded from:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/alecjacobson/common-3d-test-models/master/data/teapot.obj

That repository cites the same original source:
ftp://ftp.funet.fi/pub/sci/graphics/packages/objects/teaset.tar.Z

This copy has only `v` and `f` lines (already triangulated, no `vt`,
no `vn`) — Mesh::from_obj derives normals for it via recompute_normals.
